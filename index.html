About Peach & Avo

A husband-and-wife venture built around practical ideas

Peach & Avo is a venture founded by Billy and Fiona after their move from Hong Kong to the UK. It brings together Billy’s background in software, agile delivery and product building with Fiona’s experience in education, her keen eye for detail and her growing creative practice.

The partnership combines delivery structure with curiosity, communication and design sensibility. Together, Billy and Fiona develop services, products and ventures that use technology thoughtfully and turn promising ideas into useful, well-made outcomes—with an international outlook.
